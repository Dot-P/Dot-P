## 🔥 Research / Projects

- **Active Vision for Manipulation**\
  I am working on active perception for robots, where robots do not only recognize the scene from a given viewpoint, but also actively move their viewpoint to acquire information needed for manipulation.

- **3D Scene Affordance Segmentation**\
  During my undergraduate research, I worked on 3D scene affordance segmentation using point clouds. I am interested in estimating not only what objects exist in a scene, but also where and what kinds of actions are possible.

- **Moonshot Goal 1**\
  I am involved in a project that aims to realize an avatar-symbiotic society where people can act beyond the constraints of body, brain, space, and time.

- **KUPAC / Kyoto University Physical AI Community**\
  I am working on human-like motion generation for humanoid robots using language instructions and motion retargeting techniques.

- **Campus Locker Reservation System [(tus_yuurikai_system)](https://github.com/Lit-innosence/tus_yuurikai_system)**\
